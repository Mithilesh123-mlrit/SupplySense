# SupplySense 📦
### AI-Powered Material Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization

**Team Xanthic | Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2025**

SupplySense is a proposed machine learning-based solution for forecasting material demand and improving supply chain planning, procurement, and inventory optimization. Designed around POWERGRID's material and project patterns, it aims to help planners anticipate demand, reduce shortages and overstocking, and make smarter procurement decisions.

## 💡 Our Solution

SupplySense aims to transform historical material and project data into actionable demand forecasts. By identifying trends and seasonal patterns, the solution is intended to support timely procurement, efficient inventory management, and better resource allocation.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Demand Forecasting:** Predict future material requirements using machine learning.
- **Inventory Optimization:** Help minimize overstocking and material shortages.
- **Real-Time Dashboard:** Present forecasts, key performance indicators, and alerts.
- **Procurement Recommendations:** Support decisions about what to order, when to order, and how much to order.
- **Shortage Risk Analysis:** Prioritize critical materials to help prevent project delays.
- **What-If Simulations:** Explore alternative demand and procurement scenarios.
- **Automated Reporting:** Generate insights and recommendations for planners and managers.
- **ERP and Project Data Integration:** Designed to work with relevant enterprise and project data.

## 🛠️ Technical Approach

The proposed solution focuses on:

1. Collecting and analyzing historical material consumption and project data.
2. Identifying demand trends, seasonal patterns, and relevant factors.
3. Applying machine learning and time-series forecasting techniques.
4. Generating material demand predictions and inventory insights.
5. Displaying forecasts, shortage risks, and key metrics through a dashboard.
6. Supporting procurement planning through actionable recommendations.

*The final technology stack and model selection will depend on implementation and validation.*

## 📊 Expected Impact

- Reduce material shortages and excess inventory.
- Improve procurement planning and budget utilization.
- Minimize project delays caused by material unavailability.
- Support data-driven decisions and resource allocation.
- Enable scalable material planning across projects and regions.

## 🏗️ Feasibility and Scalability

SupplySense is proposed as a scalable solution that can begin with selected critical materials and expand to cover additional materials, projects, and regions. Integration with existing systems and forecasting accuracy will need to be validated during implementation.
